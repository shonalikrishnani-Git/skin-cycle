Skincare that follows your cycle.

Track your routine and how your skin feels, and get simple, safe guidance for each week of your month — what to hydrate and moisturise with, what to go easy on, and home care that has actually been checked against a real source before it ever reaches the screen.

Portfolio prototype, not released. General skincare information, not medical advice or contraception. "Skin Cycle" is a working title — a rename is on the list before this goes any further. Everything you enter stays on your own device; there's no account and no server.

What it does

Today opens on what your skin tends to do this week and why, three tiles for hydrate / moisturise / go easy on (worded differently for oily, dry, combination and sensitive skin — not just one generic tip), and one home-care idea. Below it: your morning and evening routine, how your skin feels, tags, and a note.

Guide covers all four phases: your routine with the reason for each step, home care with an honest evidence level and a caution on every remedy, a "don't try this at home" list, a "heard online — not quite true" myth-buster, and a Sources page tracing every claim to what backs it.

Diary is a calendar tinted by phase, with a face on each day you rate your skin, and a plain sentence about your own pattern once there's enough data to say something true.

Built to a QC standard, not a vibe

Every health claim in the guidance had to survive being treated as wrong until a real, opened source backed it — AAD, NHS, HSE, DermNet, the FDA, published studies. That process caught real problems along the way (an incomplete pregnancy note, an under-urgent warning sign, a mislabelled source) and fixed every one. 66 sources back the guidance today, and 243 automated tests now catch a missing caution, an unused source, or a stale count on their own — the full validation history is on the story page →

Who made this

Sonali

Set the direction — skin first, the cycle as context — and the rules the guidance has to follow: ingredient types not brands, no "toxic" scores, no scraped reviews, a caution on every remedy. Owned the validation and made every call on what to cut and keep.

Claude Code

An AI coding assistant. Wrote the code, ran the research and validation agents, and applied their findings.
